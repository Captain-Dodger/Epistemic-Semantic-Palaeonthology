# Research Notes

## Central methodological intuition

The word is a probe. Etymology is a surviving sensor log. The research target is the changing human meaning-space that may have left those traces.

The skill therefore links linguistic evidence to conceptual, material, social, and institutional contact surfaces without assuming that a plausible link is a proven causal link.

## Why the evidence graph matters

A sparse historical region may reflect poor preservation rather than low semantic differentiation. Always model the current state of evidence separately from the hypothesized historical meaning graph.

## Why hypotheses remain visible

Discovery suffers when every uncertain connection is deleted. Retain promising connections as labeled hypotheses. Open edges and competing branches are informative.

## Modern calibration opportunities

Recent language change can provide unusually dense evidence: dated corpora, archived forums, mass media, dictionaries, generational testimony, and language-contact records. Modern borrowings can therefore help calibrate how semantic integration, resistance, and stabilization appear in observable data before applying analogous patterns cautiously to older periods.
